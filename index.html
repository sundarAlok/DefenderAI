01 · Quest Proposal

From fragmented data to actionable intelligence

CrisisTensor is a Bittensor subnet designed to create a decentralized disaster-intelligence network. It transforms satellite imagery, weather feeds, seismic activity, flood sensors, emergency reports, news, and public signals into structured intelligence about what is happening, where risk is increasing, and which areas may need priority attention.

The Problem

During floods, wildfires, earthquakes, hurricanes, and other disasters, critical information is fragmented across many systems. Emergency teams need reliable situational awareness quickly, but manual analysis can be slow, inconsistent, and difficult to scale.

The Solution

Validators create real-world intelligence tasks. Independent miners use specialized AI systems to analyze the same evidence and return predictions, classifications, severity estimates, and risk signals. Validators benchmark those outputs against verified data, consensus methods, and later outcomes.

The Opportunity

The world has data.
The problem is intelligence.

During disasters, the information needed for decisions is scattered across systems that do not naturally operate as one intelligence layer.

  • Satellite imagery provides visual evidence, but requires interpretation.
  • Weather, seismic and sensor data reveal changing conditions.
  • Reports and public signals add ground-level context.
  • Responders need one coherent picture instead of dozens of disconnected feeds.
